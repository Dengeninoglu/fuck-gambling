# The FU#K!! G#MBLING Manifesto

*[Türkçe](MANIFESTO.tr.md)*

**This app was built within a few hours of realising how much money I had spent on gambling.**
If you find any error or bug, please message me: [open an issue](https://github.com/Dengeninoglu/fuck-gambling/issues).

---

### 1. Gambling is designed to win. You are not.
Betting apps are built by teams of engineers and psychologists whose job is to keep you playing: bonuses, "free bets", push notifications at 2 a.m., new mirror sites the day the old one is blocked. Losing to that is not a character flaw. It is the product working as designed.

### 2. Protection should cost nothing.
The people who need a blocker most are often the people who have just lost their money. Asking them for a monthly subscription is wrong. FU#K!! G#MBLING is **free, forever**. No premium tier, no ads, no data collection, no account with us.

### 3. Block gambling, not your life.
A blocker that also blocks your bank, your crypto exchange or your work gets switched off. Ours blocks betting and casino sites **only**. Banking, payments, your adult life and **help & recovery sites** are none of our business, and the way out must never be blocked. Where the only free no-account option also blocks adult sites, we say so plainly and let you choose.

### 4. Design for the worst moment.
Nobody uninstalls a blocker on a good day. So removing protection takes **72 hours**, and we tell you to give the passcode to someone you trust. And because nobody sets up a blocker if it's complicated, setup takes a few minutes and a QR code. The goal is to outlast the urge, not to trap you. Everything is reversible and nothing can damage your device.

### 5. Open, so you can trust it.
Every line of code is public (MIT license). Anyone can check what it does, translate it, improve it or hand it out. Organisations that fight gambling harm can use it, rebrand it and give it away for free.

### 6. Honest about the limits.
No blocker stops someone who is determined. A VPN can get around DNS filters, and new mirror sites appear every day. We say this out loud, block what we can (including VPN tricks where possible), and put the real defence where it belongs: the people around you.

### 7. Technology buys time. People heal.
A blocker gives you hours and days back. Use them: call a helpline, tell someone, ask your bank to block gambling payments, join a group. You are not alone, and you are not the first to get out.
