# iPhone: make it stick

1. **Delete gambling apps.**
2. **Set up the filter:** open https://dengeninoglu.github.io/fuck-gambling/iphone/ in Safari (or scan the QR in the app) and choose **A) gambling only** (free NextDNS account) or **B) no account** (also blocks adult sites).
   Then Settings → General → VPN, DNS & Device Management → **DNS** → select *FU#K!! G#MBLING*.
   Test: a betting site should not open; your bank should.
3. **Private Relay OFF:** Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Private Relay → Off (otherwise Safari can skip the filter).
4. **Screen Time passcode, set by someone you trust:** Settings → Screen Time → Lock Screen Time Settings. Hand them the phone and let them choose the code.
5. **Content & Privacy Restrictions** (with their code):
   - App Store, Media, Web & Games → Web Content → *Limit Adult Websites*; add betting sites under *Never Allow*.
   - iTunes & App Store Purchases → Installing Apps → *Don't Allow* (they unlock it when you need a new app).
   - Allowed changes: **VPN & DNS**, **Account Changes**, **Passcode Changes** → *Don't Allow* (where your iOS version shows them).

The profile can still be removed in Settings unless step 5 locks it. The Screen Time passcode in someone else's hands does most of the work.
